JC Automations

Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

1. About JC Automations

JC Automations is an AI-powered application that automatically generates short-form videos and publishes them to social media platforms including TikTok, YouTube Shorts, and Facebook Reels. This Privacy Policy explains how JC Automations collects, uses, and protects your information.

2. Information We Collect

JC Automations collects minimal information necessary to operate the Service:

  • TikTok account credentials and OAuth tokens obtained through TikTok's official Login Kit
  • Content metadata generated by JC Automations (video titles, descriptions, hashtags)
  • Performance metrics retrieved from TikTok and other connected platforms

3. TikTok API Usage

JC Automations integrates with the TikTok Content Posting API to publish AI-generated videos on your behalf. By connecting your TikTok account to JC Automations, you authorize the application to:

  • Upload and publish video content to your TikTok account (video.upload, video.publish)
  • Access basic account information (user.info.basic)

JC Automations only requests permissions strictly necessary for content publishing. TikTok data usage is governed by TikTok's Privacy Policy.

4. How We Use Your Information

JC Automations uses collected information solely to:

  • Generate and publish AI-created videos on your behalf via TikTok's API
  • Analyze content performance to improve future video suggestions
  • Renew OAuth access tokens to maintain uninterrupted publishing

5. Data Storage

All data processed by JC Automations is stored locally on your self-hosted infrastructure. JC Automations does not transmit or store your personal data on external servers. You maintain full control over your data at all times.

6. Data Sharing

JC Automations does not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. Data is only shared with TikTok and other connected platforms as required to provide the content publishing service.

7. Data Retention

You control how long data is retained within JC Automations. You may revoke JC Automations' access to your TikTok account at any time through TikTok's account settings.

8. Security

JC Automations implements appropriate technical measures to protect your OAuth tokens and account credentials. Access tokens are stored securely and refreshed automatically using TikTok's refresh token mechanism.

9. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete any personal data held by JC Automations. You may disconnect your TikTok account from JC Automations at any time.

10. Contact

For privacy-related questions about JC Automations, please contact us through the GitHub repository at github.com/joca25/n8n_tiktok.
