Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

1. Information We Collect

Colorchemy collects minimal information necessary to operate the Service:

  • Account credentials for connected social media platforms
  • Content metadata (video titles, descriptions, hashtags)
  • Performance metrics from connected platforms

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information solely to:

  • Generate and publish AI-created video content on your behalf
  • Analyze content performance to improve future suggestions
  • Maintain and improve the Service

3. Data Storage

All data is stored locally on your self-hosted infrastructure. Colorchemy does not store your data on external servers. You maintain full control over your data at all times.

4. Third-Party Integrations

The Service connects to TikTok, YouTube, Facebook, Google Drive, and Telegram. Data shared with these platforms is governed by their respective privacy policies. We only request permissions necessary for content publishing and analytics.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We do not share your data with any external parties except as required by law.

6. Data Retention

You control how long data is retained. You may delete your data at any time by clearing your local database.

7. Security

We implement appropriate technical measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete any personal data we hold about you. Contact us at any time to exercise these rights.

9. Contact

For privacy-related questions, please contact us at the email address associated with your account.
