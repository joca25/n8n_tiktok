2. Description of JC Automations

3. TikTok Integration

JC Automations integrates with TikTok through TikTok's official developer platform. By connecting your TikTok account to JC Automations, you grant the application permission to publish video content and access basic account information on your behalf. JC Automations uses the following TikTok API scopes:
